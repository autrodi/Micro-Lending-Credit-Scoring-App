# https://score-me-micro-credit-app.streamlit.app/
A micro-lending credit scoring app that leverages random forest regression model to develop behavioural risk-based assessment; trained on 23,000 records of synthetic data to bridge credit assessment gaps when determining micro-lending metrices of potential applicants.

The Credit Scoring App relies on 9 questions only to evaluate a credit score (with constraints typically at 300 and 850) that summarizes local income parity, dependency ratio, outflow ratio, betting habits and lending habits in order to ascertain potential applicant's eligibility for micro-finance given baseline costs and income stability.
